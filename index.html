Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you contact us.

What does I. MASTER GROUP LTD do?

We operate through specialist companies covering digital and technology services, global business and recruitment/outsourcing solutions.

Can I request a website or software project?

Yes. Send your project idea through the contact form or WhatsApp and the technology team can review the enquiry.

Can a business ask I2 for recruitment support?

Yes. Employers can contact the group to discuss recruitment, staffing and outsourcing requirements.

Where is the group based?

The group is based in Kano State, Nigeria, with online enquiries available for customers beyond the local area.
